# Love Villa: Nations — Show Bible

**Logline.** Eight gloriously exaggerated singles from around the world share one luxury villa, one pool, and zero chill — a fictional international dating competition where the real language barrier is feelings.

**Tone.** funny · dramatic · fast-paced · absurd · addictive · serialized · playful never mean

**Visual style.** Glossy, exaggerated, semi-realistic animation. Colorful, attractive, slightly cartoonish, expressive, high-energy. Designed for vertical mobile viewing. Consistent character designs across episodes. Never photorealistic, never deepfakes.

## Episode format (60–90s vertical)

- **hook** (0-3s): Open mid-conflict/surprise. Never an intro, logo, or explanation.
- **setup** (3-15s): Establish what happened and who is involved.
- **escalation** (15-50s): Quick dialogue, reactions, confessionals, misunderstandings.
- **twist** (50-70s): Secret, arrival, betrayal, text, result, or romantic decision.
- **engagement** (final seconds): Cliffhanger, vote, question, or next-episode preview.

## Writing rules

- NARRATOR-LED: an off-screen sassy omniscient narrator tells the story in fast, punchy, present-tense gossip. ~60-70% of lines are narrator; contestants get short reaction quotes.
- Narrator sentences are SHORT (3-10 words), present tense, half-addressed to the audience ('This is Brock.' 'Watch his face.' 'She said no. Twice.').
- First line must be an irresistible hook naming a character and an imminent disaster. Never an intro, never scene-setting.
- Beats every 2-4 seconds: statement → reaction → escalation. No speeches, no filler, no dead air.
- Every episode: ≥1 laugh-out-loud beat, ≥1 romantic gasp, ≥1 betrayal-grade conflict, ≥1 face-reaction beat, ≥1 confessional quote, ≥1 unanswered question.
- Every episode connects to a previous or future episode; characters and narrator remember everything.
- Humor is bold but character-driven: we laugh at their choices, never at their nationality's worth.
- Accents are seasoning, not the joke — always fully intelligible.

## Safety rules

- All characters are fictional adults aged 21+. No real celebrities, influencers, politicians, or private individuals.
- No racial slurs, no dehumanizing language, no stereotypes of a nationality as stupid, criminal, dirty, violent, inferior, or untrustworthy.
- Every country gets approximately equal comedic treatment; every character has positives, flaws, motivations, vulnerabilities.
- No explicit sexual content; flirty and chaotic, PG-13 always.

## Brand safety (original property)

- Original property: never copy Love Island branding, logo, music, narration, catchphrases, character names, villa design, games, or plots.
- The narrator persona is ORIGINAL — never imitate any real show's narrator voice, cadence, or catchphrases.
- Only royalty-free, generated, or originally licensed music and SFX.

Banned phrases (validated on every script): "love island", "i've got a text", "i got a text", "casa amor", "mugged off", "grafting", "the hideaway", "islanders", "crack on", "my type on paper", "stick or twist", "coupled up ceremony"

## Villa Solana — Isla Corazón (fictional)

A candy-bright clifftop villa on a fictional island: curved terracotta arches, a turquoise infinity pool, neon heart sculptures, and one suspiciously well-lit confessional called The Spill Room.

- **Infinity Pool** (`pool`, day): Turquoise infinity pool with pink loungers and a swim-up heart-shaped bar.
- **Firepit Lounge** (`firepit`, sunset): Sunken circular firepit with amber cushions — where the serious conversations combust.
- **Open Kitchen** (`kitchen`, day): Marble island kitchen with copper pans — Matteo's kingdom and Rohan's laboratory.
- **Shared Bedroom** (`bedroom`, night): One giant room, eight beds, zero privacy — where whispers travel at the speed of gossip.
- **The Spill Room** (`confessional`, night): A neon-lit velvet confessional booth where contestants tell the camera the truth-ish.
- **Sunset Terrace** (`terrace`, sunset): A bougainvillea-draped rooftop terrace — the villa's official overthinking zone.
- **Entrance Walkway** (`walkway`, night): A torch-lit palm walkway where new arrivals strut in — nobody good ever walks in quietly.
