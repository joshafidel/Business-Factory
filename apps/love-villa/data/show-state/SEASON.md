# Season 1 — Love Villa: Nations

**Theme.** Eight nations, one villa, and the world's least diplomatic summit on love.

A love triangle over Élodie detonates on day one and is immediately complicated by Spanish latecomer Alejandro. Across ten episodes: Brock's bravado cracks and Poppy accidentally defends him; Rohan's over-planning collides with Sienna's chaos; Greta schedules her way into Matteo's kitchen and heart; Lucas's pool secret and Poppy's poetry notebook both surface; Alejandro's strategy is exposed; and the finale locks in couples while planting season 2.

Review this file for the "season" approval checkpoint.

## Episode 1: Two Kings, One Croissant

- **Hook:** America and Italy just declared war over France.
- **Twist:** New arrival: Alejandro from Spain walks in and says he's here for Élodie.
- **Engagement:** Who should Élodie pick? 🇺🇸🇮🇹🇪🇸 Vote in the comments!
- **Focus:** brock-usa, matteo-italy, elodie-france, poppy-uk, greta-germany
- **Connects:** Series premiere — plants the triangle, the rivalry, and Alejandro's arrival.

Brock and Matteo simultaneously discover they are both pursuing Élodie. Smoothie diplomacy and an eight-course revenge risotto escalate into the villa's first international incident, while Élodie rates both suitors a five. A torch-lit twist ends day one: Spain has entered the villa — for Élodie.

## Episode 2: The Spanish Inquisition of Hearts

- **Hook:** The new guy just asked Élodie to dance — in front of BOTH of them.
- **Twist:** Élodie rates Alejandro a six — a villa record — then immediately regrets revealing it.
- **Engagement:** Is Alejandro genuine or just smooth? 👀
- **Focus:** alejandro-spain, elodie-france, brock-usa, matteo-italy, lucas-brazil
- **Connects:** Direct continuation of Alejandro's episode 1 arrival.

Alejandro's first morning: he says eleven words total and destabilizes the entire villa. Brock responds with a louder blender; Matteo cooks a threatening breakfast. Élodie gives Alejandro a six — the highest score ever recorded — and the villa loses its mind.

## Episode 3: Heartquake

- **Hook:** One quiz question just ended two friendships and started a romance.
- **Twist:** Challenge result: the algorithm loses, the chaos couple wins — Greta chooses Matteo for the prize dinner.
- **Engagement:** Greta + Matteo dinner date... what could go wrong? 🍝📋
- **Focus:** rohan-india, sienna-australia, greta-germany, matteo-italy, brock-usa
- **Connects:** Sienna's ep2 teasing of Rohan escalates into the challenge dare.

First villa challenge: 'Heartquake' — couples answer questions about each other while standing on a wobble platform over the pool. Rohan's spreadsheet fails him; Sienna dares him into the best answer of the day. Greta and Matteo accidentally win everything, to their mutual horror and delight.

## Episode 4: Dinner at Eight, Feelings at Nine

- **Hook:** Greta scheduled a 90-minute date. Matteo cooked for six hours. Nobody's ready.
- **Twist:** Matteo realizes mid-tiramisu that he didn't think about Élodie once all night.
- **Engagement:** Greta+Matteo or Élodie+Alejandro — which date won the night? 🗳️
- **Focus:** greta-germany, matteo-italy, elodie-france, alejandro-spain
- **Connects:** The ep3 challenge prize plays out; Élodie continues testing Alejandro from ep2.

The prize dinner: Greta arrives with an agenda; Matteo arrives with eight courses and unresolved Élodie feelings. Across the villa, Élodie stress-tests Alejandro's mystique and finds one crack: he laughed — really laughed — at her rating system. Both pairs end closer than they planned.

## Episode 5: The Lemonade Papers

- **Hook:** Brock's 'seven-figure empire' just got fact-checked at the firepit.
- **Twist:** Secret revealed: the empire is a lemonade stand — and Poppy is the one who saves him.
- **Engagement:** Poppy defending Brock?? What is HAPPENING 👀
- **Focus:** brock-usa, poppy-uk, rohan-india, lucas-brazil
- **Connects:** Brock's ep2 confidence crack finally splits open; Rohan's rivalry with flair over data.

Rohan's innocent networking question unravels Brock's startup story in front of everyone. The villa braces to laugh — and Poppy, of all people, torches the pile-on instead. Brock's honest firepit speech about being broke and hopeful becomes his most genuinely liked moment ever.

## Episode 6: The Vote of No Confidence

- **Hook:** The villa voted. Someone's suitcase is already on the walkway.
- **Twist:** No one leaves — instead a sealed vote result now hangs over every future couple.
- **Engagement:** WHOSE name is in the envelope? Wrong answers only 😈
- **Focus:** poppy-uk, lucas-brazil, alejandro-spain, sienna-australia
- **Connects:** The ep1 vote engagement pays off; alliances seeded in ep5 solidify.

First elimination vote — viewers chose the 'least matched' contestant. The suitcase on the walkway belongs to... nobody: it's a twist. The vote result is sealed in an envelope that will only open when a couple officially forms. The villa turns on itself guessing whose name is inside.

## Episode 7: Deep End Diplomacy

- **Hook:** Lucas just refused a pool party. LUCAS.
- **Twist:** Lucas tells Poppy his secret — and Poppy responds with sincerity, her rarest resource.
- **Engagement:** Poppy was NICE. On purpose. Ship or panic? 🚢
- **Focus:** lucas-brazil, poppy-uk, alejandro-spain, brock-usa
- **Connects:** The ep6 safety-hand becomes real trust; Alejandro-Lucas cold war from ep2 heats up.

Villa pool olympics. Lucas — sunshine incarnate — dodges every water event until Alejandro smells weakness and calls a cannonball contest. Poppy spots the truth, fakes a cramp, and quietly gets the party moved to the shallow end. Lucas confesses his secret to her at the firepit; she doesn't joke. Not once.

## Episode 8: The Notebook Situation

- **Hook:** Someone found Poppy's poetry. And read it. OUT LOUD.
- **Twist:** Secret revealed: Poppy's poetry — and everyone now knows who it's about.
- **Engagement:** Lucas learned the poem BY HEART. What's he going to do with it?? 📖
- **Focus:** poppy-uk, sienna-australia, lucas-brazil, greta-germany
- **Connects:** Pays off Poppy's ep1-established notebook secret and ep7 firepit closeness.

Bedroom chaos: Sienna, hunting for sunscreen, finds Poppy's poetry notebook and — not knowing whose it is — performs a dramatic reading at breakfast. The poem is unmistakably about a boy who dances. Poppy claims it's 'satire'. Lucas quietly memorizes every word. Sienna realizes what she's done and, for the first time ever, apologizes without a dare attached.

## Episode 9: The Ten Out of Ten Conspiracy

- **Hook:** Alejandro has a list. Élodie's name is at the top. It's not a love list.
- **Twist:** Betrayal inverted: the 'strategy list' ends with Alejandro genuinely off-script about Élodie.
- **Engagement:** Can Élodie trust him now? YES or RUN 🏃‍♀️
- **Focus:** alejandro-spain, elodie-france, greta-germany, matteo-italy, brock-usa
- **Connects:** Alejandro's ep2 mystique and ep7 edge finally get an origin story.

Greta, reorganizing the kitchen at 06:00, finds Alejandro's audition notebook: four years of notes on how to win villa shows — charm arcs, target selection, 'the French one: highest value'. The villa splits: is it calculated, or is it four years of wanting to be loved with a spreadsheet? Élodie reads the notebook alone at the firepit... and finds the last page: her name, and next to it, no strategy. Just 'actually funny. actually kind. off-script.'

## Episode 10: The Envelope, Please

- **Hook:** First official couple. Which opens the envelope. From episode six. Tonight.
- **Twist:** The envelope names Alejandro — and Élodie chooses him anyway, sort of, maybe, a seven.
- **Engagement:** Season 2: new flags on the horizon. Which countries should board? 🌍
- **Focus:** lucas-brazil, poppy-uk, alejandro-spain, elodie-france, greta-germany
- **Connects:** Resolves ep6 envelope, ep8 poem, ep9 notebook; launches season 2.

Finale night. Lucas answers the poem — with a dance, obviously, then with words. Poppy says yes in plain English, no joke, a villa first. That makes them the first official couple — which unseals the ep6 envelope. The vote inside named the 'least matched' contestant: Alejandro. He stands to leave; Élodie stands up too — 'the vote was before the last page.' Season ends on Greta's clipboard: 'Season 2 agenda, item one: everything is unresolved.'
