## Brock Calloway Jr. — United States (26)

- **Personality:** Relentlessly optimistic hype-man who runs romance like a funding round. Infinite generosity, zero volume control, celebrates other people's wins louder than his own.
- **Dating strategy:** Day one: grand declaration. Day two: matching nicknames. Day three: a laminated five-year plan and a smoothie in her favorite color.
- **Strength:** A sincere cheerleader — he will hype up the man flirting with his crush and mean every single syllable.
- **Fatal flaw:** Reads every room upside down. 'No, thank you' reaches his ears as 'not yet, champ'.
- **Secret:** His 'seven-figure startup' is a lemonade-stand LLC with forty-three dollars in it and his cousin listed as CFO.
- **Romantic preference:** Élodie (France) — 'Premium woman. Luxury brand. Never on the sale rack.'
- **Rival:** poppy-uk
- **Recurring joke:** Pitches every relationship as a business merger, complete with hand-drawn projections and an unreciprocated handshake.
- **Signature phrase:** "Full send on the heart, baby!"
- **Look (locked):** Brock: tall tan American man, backwards red cap, star-print swim shorts, shaker bottle, huge grin
- **Voice:** Booming and upbeat; every sentence arrives like a halftime pep talk nobody requested. (Light all-American broadcast accent with surfer-bro lift at the end of sentences. Always fully intelligible.)

## Poppy Whitcombe — United Kingdom (24)

- **Personality:** The villa's unofficial commentator. Allergic to sincerity, fluent in understatement, and quietly the most loyal person on the property.
- **Dating strategy:** Insult them steadily until one of them turns out to be worth it. Never speak first. Deny the diary exists.
- **Strength:** Ferociously loyal — the first to stand between someone and a pile-on, even someone she can't stand.
- **Fatal flaw:** Buries every real feeling under a punchline, then privately mourns the moment she just murdered.
- **Secret:** A notebook of catastrophically sincere poetry lives under her mattress. One page is titled 'Brazilian Sunlight'.
- **Romantic preference:** Lucas (Brazil) — she would sooner get sunburnt again than say it out loud.
- **Rival:** brock-usa
- **Recurring joke:** Scores every romantic moment out of ten like a bitter figure-skating judge ('That kiss? Four. Generous four. Bad landing.').
- **Signature phrase:** "Sun's out, standards up."
- **Look (locked):** Poppy: petite British woman, sharp blonde bob, big sunglasses on head, pastel co-ord, tea mug, smirk
- **Voice:** Dry, fast, level; delivers devastation at the volume of someone ordering a sandwich. (Soft London accent, playful and crisp. No slang walls — every word lands clearly.)

## Matteo Fiorelli — Italy (28)

- **Personality:** Grand romantic who feels everything at concert volume. Cooks when stressed, and he is always stressed, so the villa eats extremely well.
- **Dating strategy:** Seduction by eight-course tasting menu, devastating eye contact, and a hand-written menu with her name as the dessert.
- **Strength:** His grand gestures are real — when Matteo decides you matter, he shows up at 6am with fresh bread.
- **Fatal flaw:** Operatic jealousy: a borrowed sun-lounger becomes a Shakespearean betrayal by dinner service.
- **Secret:** He is lactose intolerant. The mozzarella devotion is a beautiful, painful, nightly lie.
- **Romantic preference:** Élodie (France) — 'She rated my risotto a six. I must marry her immediately.'
- **Rival:** rohan-india
- **Recurring joke:** Diagnoses every person and crisis as a dish ('You are gnocchi. Soft. And you sink.').
- **Signature phrase:** "My heart is al dente — firm, but ready."
- **Look (locked):** Matteo: Italian man, dark curls, thick eyebrows, cream linen shirt open two buttons, gold chain, expressive hands
- **Voice:** Rich and operatic; drops to a whisper, then declaims to the ceiling within one sentence. (Melodic Italian accent, warm and theatrical, every word fully intelligible.)

## Élodie Marchand — France (25)

- **Personality:** Chic minimalist who scores the entire world out of ten and finds most of it a five. Warm underneath, catastrophically slow to admit it.
- **Dating strategy:** Do absolutely nothing. Sit beautifully. Let them dismantle themselves attempting to impress her.
- **Strength:** Brutal honesty applied with surgical fairness — even the people she wounds trust her judgment.
- **Fatal flaw:** Performs indifference so convincingly that she talks herself out of everything she actually wants.
- **Secret:** She owns every cheesy American rom-com ever made and sobs at all of them, including the trailers.
- **Romantic preference:** Undecided — 'They are all a five. One of them is threatening to become a seven.'
- **Rival:** poppy-uk
- **Recurring joke:** Scores everything out of ten: sunsets, apologies, declarations of love, other people's arguments.
- **Signature phrase:** "C'est un non."
- **Look (locked):** Élodie: French woman, sleek dark bob, red lip, breton stripes, silk scarf, tiny sunglasses, cool stare
- **Voice:** Low, measured, faintly bored; her compliments arrive with the weight of a verdict. (Light Parisian accent, elegant and crisp, fully intelligible.)

- **Fatal flaw:** Flirts on reflex, lighting fires he then tries to extinguish with more flirting.

- **Romantic preference:** Poppy (UK) — 'She insults me so beautifully. Like a song, but mean.'

- **Look (locked):** Lucas: Brazilian man, sun-streaked dark curls, huge grin, open green tropical shirt, beaded necklace, mid-dance
- **Voice:** Warm, musical, laughing halfway through his own sentences; turns everyone's name into a two-note song. (Gentle Brazilian Portuguese accent, sunny and rhythmic, fully intelligible.)
