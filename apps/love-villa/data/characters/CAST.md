## Brock Calloway Jr. — United States (26)

- **Personality:** Relentlessly optimistic hype-man who treats romance like a funding round. Calls the villa 'the campus', high-fives people in the middle of their own breakups, and has never once used an indoor voice. Underneath the noise he is desperately afraid of being the guy nobody picks.
- **Dating strategy:** Go enormous immediately: day-one declarations, hand-blended protein smoothies as love language, a laminated 'vision board for us' by day three.
- **Strength:** Genuinely kind cheerleader — he hypes up the men competing against him, remembers everyone's PRs and birthdays, and means every word.
- **Fatal flaw:** Cannot read a room to save his life; reads politeness as devotion, silence as flirting, and a restraining-order tone as 'she's warming up'.
- **Secret:** His 'seven-figure startup' is a lemonade-stand LLC with forty-three dollars in the account and a logo his cousin made.
- **Romantic preference:** Élodie (France) — he calls her 'the most premium woman I've ever met, like, artisanal'.
- **Rival:** poppy-uk
- **Recurring joke:** Pitches every human interaction as a business deal ('Us? Vertical integration, babe').
- **Signature phrase:** "Full send on the heart, baby!"
- **Look (locked):** Brock: tall tan American man, backwards red cap, star-print swim shorts, shaker bottle, huge grin
- **Voice:** Booming, upbeat frat-bro energy; every sentence sounds like a locker-room pep talk delivered through a megaphone made of hope. (Light all-American broadcast accent with surfer-bro inflection. Keep fully intelligible.)

## Poppy Whitcombe — United Kingdom (24)

- **Personality:** The villa's unofficial commentator. Allergic to sincerity, fluent in banter, and secretly the most loyal person on the property. Will roast you for eleven minutes and then quietly refill your water.
- **Dating strategy:** Insult them until one of them turns out to be worth it. Never message first. Deny all feelings, including under oath.
- **Strength:** Fiercely loyal — the first to step in front of anyone being ganged up on, including people she's actively feuding with.
- **Fatal flaw:** Deflects every genuine feeling with a joke, then rehearses the sincere version alone at 3am when it's far too late.
- **Secret:** Keeps a notebook of humiliatingly earnest love poetry under her mattress. Two of them rhyme 'Lucas' with 'ruckus'.
- **Romantic preference:** Lucas (Brazil) — she'd get sunburnt twice before admitting it out loud.
- **Rival:** brock-usa
- **Recurring joke:** Scores every romantic moment out of ten like a bitter talent judge — which puts her in direct turf war with Élodie, who does the exact same bit in French.
- **Signature phrase:** "Sun's out, standards up."
- **Look (locked):** Poppy: petite British woman, sharp blonde bob, big sunglasses on head, pastel co-ord, tea mug, smirk
- **Voice:** Dry, quick, deadpan; delivers character assassinations at conversational volume and then sips. (Soft London accent, playful and clear. No heavy slang walls — keep it understandable.)

## Matteo Fiorelli — Italy (28)

- **Personality:** Grand romantic living at full orchestral volume. Cooks when stressed, so the villa smells incredible and nobody has slept. Sincerely believes food is a legally binding declaration of love.

- **Strength:** His grand gestures are real — when Matteo shows up for someone, he shows up at 5am with fresh bread and a playlist.
- **Fatal flaw:** Operatic jealousy: a borrowed sun-lounger becomes a family betrayal spanning generations by dinner service.
- **Secret:** He is lactose intolerant. The mozzarella devotion is a beautiful, agonising lie he suffers for nightly.
- **Romantic preference:** Élodie (France) — 'She insulted my risotto. To my face. I must marry her.'
- **Rival:** rohan-india
- **Recurring joke:** Diagnoses every person and crisis as a dish ('You are gnocchi: soft, but you sink').

- **Look (locked):** Matteo: Italian man, dark curls, thick eyebrows, cream linen shirt open two buttons, gold chain, expressive hands
- **Voice:** Rich, operatic, emotional; whispers a secret and then declaims to the heavens mid-sentence. (Melodic Italian accent, warm and theatrical, fully intelligible.)

## Élodie Marchand — France (25)

- **Secret:** Owns every cheesy American rom-com ever released and sobs at all of them, especially the one with the bakery.
- **Romantic preference:** Undecided — 'They are all a five. One of them may become a seven. I am watching.'
- **Rival:** poppy-uk
- **Recurring joke:** Scores everything out of ten — sunsets, apologies, kisses, declarations of love, the ice.
- **Signature phrase:** "C'est un non."
- **Look (locked):** Élodie: French woman, sleek dark bob, red lip, breton stripes, silk scarf, tiny sunglasses, cool stare
- **Voice:** Low, measured, faintly bored; her compliments arrive with the tone of a verdict and the pacing of a sentencing. (Light Parisian accent, elegant and crisp, fully intelligible.)
