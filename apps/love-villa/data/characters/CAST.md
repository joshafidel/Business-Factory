## Brock Calloway Jr. — United States (26)

- **Personality:** Relentlessly optimistic hype-man who treats romance like a startup pitch and heartbreak like a Q3 setback. Loud, generous, zero volume control, hugs like a forklift.
- **Dating strategy:** Go big on day one: grand declarations before breakfast, protein smoothies as love language, a five-year plan for a girl he met four minutes ago.
- **Strength:** Genuinely kind cheerleader — he hypes up even the man stealing his girl, and means every word of it.
- **Fatal flaw:** Cannot read a room with subtitles on; mistakes politeness for love, silence for chemistry, and a restraining-order tone for flirting.
- **Secret:** His 'seven-figure startup' is a lemonade-stand LLC with $43 in the bank and one investor: his mom.
- **Romantic preference:** Élodie (France) — he calls her 'the most premium woman I've ever met', which she rates a four.
- **Rival:** poppy-uk
- **Recurring joke:** Pitches every relationship as a business deal, complete with imaginary slide deck ('Us? Vertical integration, babe').
- **Signature phrase:** "Full send on the heart, baby!"
- **Look (locked):** Brock: tall tan American man, American-flag tank top (stars and stripes), red backwards cap with white star, star-print shorts, shaker bottle, huge grin
- **Voice:** Booming, upbeat frat-bro energy; every sentence sounds like a locker-room pep talk delivered through a megaphone made of hope. (Light all-American broadcast accent with surfer-bro inflection. Keep fully intelligible.)
